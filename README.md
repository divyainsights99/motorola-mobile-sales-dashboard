# 📊 Motorola Mobile Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Motorola Mobile Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze mobile sales performance across India.

The dashboard enables users to monitor KPIs, analyze sales trends, evaluate customer ratings, compare payment methods, and identify top-performing brands and mobile models through interactive visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyze total mobile sales performance.
- Monitor sales quantity and transactions.
- Compare sales across different cities.
- Identify top-selling mobile models.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Study preferred payment methods.
- Enable month-wise performance tracking.
- Provide actionable insights for business decisions.

# 📂 Dashboard Features

### 📈 KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important business KPIs including:

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold
- 🛒 Total Transactions
- 💲 Average Selling Price

---

### 🗺️ City-wise Sales Analysis

An interactive Bing Map visualizes sales distribution across major Indian cities such as:

- Delhi
- Mumbai
- Kolkata
- Chennai
- Bengaluru
- Hyderabad
- Lucknow
- Patna
- Ranchi
- Indore
- Ludhiana
- Coimbatore

This visualization helps identify geographical sales performance.

---

### 📅 Daily Sales Trend

A Line Chart displays daily sales quantity throughout the month.

Business users can identify:

- Peak sales days
- Low-performing days
- Sales fluctuations
- Customer purchasing behavior

---

### ⭐ Customer Ratings Analysis

The dashboard analyzes customer satisfaction using ratings from 1 to 5.

This helps understand:

- Customer experience
- Product quality perception
- Service performance

---

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

Transactions are categorized into:

- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- Cash

The pie chart helps determine the most preferred payment option.

---

### 🏆 Brand Performance

A comparison table shows:

- Brand
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Transactions

Brands included:

- Apple
- Samsung
- Vivo
- Xiaomi
- OnePlus

---

### 📱 Top Selling Models

Bar charts identify the highest-selling smartphone models.

This enables businesses to:

- Monitor product demand
- Optimize inventory
- Improve marketing strategy

---

### 📆 Month Filter

Users can dynamically filter dashboard insights by selecting any month from January to December.

---

### 📱 Mobile Model Filter

Allows users to analyze sales for individual smartphone models.

---

### 💳 Payment Method Filter

Provides dynamic filtering based on payment mode.
